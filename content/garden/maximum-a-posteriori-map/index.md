## TLDR {#tldr}

No not map, MAP. Look math is hard, naming things is hard it is called MAP leave me alone.

MAP is maximum a posteriori estimation, in <a href="/garden/bayesian-statistics/">Bayesian Statistics</a>. It is the maximum value of a distribution given observation. I.e. it can tell you an estimate of the most likely outcome under the current model given previous observations. MAP is one of the most used <a href="/garden/inference-queries/">Inference Queries</a> in <a href="/garden/bayesian-statistics/">Bayesian Statistics</a>.
